01 — LOOK FOR A DIP

Watch a star get dimmer.

A transit briefly lowers a star’s measured brightness. Look for repeated dips with similar depths and spacing.

02 — STACK THE SIGNAL

Bring the pattern into focus.

Phase folding wraps observations around the known orbital period. Repeating transits line up at phase zero.

03 — CHECK YOUR HUNCH
